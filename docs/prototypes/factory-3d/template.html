What this shows

  • The data is already there. Every building and belt in a snapshot has its height, belts have their curves in 3D, and the Docs file's clearance boxes give each machine's shape in 3D. Nothing new is read from the save.
  • The cut-off answers "is the top floor starved, or the whole factory?" Slide it down and look from above: you see one floor's machines at a time.
  • Splitters, mergers and lifts have no clearance boxes in the Docs file, so they're small cubes here; a lift's real height would come from the save.
  • For a factory, the viewer would show its members, traced from its definition, instead of an area cut out of the map.
